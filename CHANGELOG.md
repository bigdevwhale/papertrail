## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- The office: the matchbook with today's advance (the monthly advance minus bills, spread over the days to payday), a match for each day of the period.
- Clues by hand on the typewriter, or from the informant: bank notifications read on the phone, from apps marked as banks only, sorted by place once a suspect has been there twice.
- The files: twelve house suspects and honest citizens with police photos drawn in code, plus your own; a dossier with the record by month, the hours and days they work, and a monthly tail.
- Cases that open by themselves on five patterns (night work, small change, a spree, the same weekday, a broken tail), pinned on a cork board with red thread; motive, verdict, measure and the stamp.
- Ghosts (subscriptions found by themselves), cold cases (cash with no trace) and scheduled bills kept back from the advance.
- The safe and tickets out of town with ten stamps; the city paper for every period; the evening report at nine; ranks for work done.
- EN and RU, sounds synthesised in the app, a demo build, and community links to the StillCraft bot on Telegram.
